Mozarella salad

Home

Description

An incredible, Italian inspired salad to freshen up and accompany near any meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 ball of mozarella
  • 1 big ripe tomato
  • 8-12 fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Balsamico vinegar
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Slice mozarella
  2. Slice tomato
  3. Place on a plate, intermingling the tomato with the mozarella
  4. Add salt and pepper to taste
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and vinegar
  6. Garnish with basil leaves

Notes

You shouldn't add MSG. MSG does not make this salad better.

If you can't be bothered with the pretty presentation, just dice everything and toss in a bowl together, it'll still taste awesome.

Btw the colours make the Italian flag, ain't that neat.
